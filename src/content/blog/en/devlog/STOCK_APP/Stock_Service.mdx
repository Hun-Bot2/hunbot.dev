## Idea
I have 1,000,000 KRW in seed money, but I am not a stock expert. I do not know which stocks to buy, how to study stocks, or how to analyze charts. Still, I do not want to lose money.

Then can AI buy and sell stocks without taking losses? Is that already in the realm of AGI? If there were an AI that never loses money, would it not be almost like a god? I want to build an AI stock trading bot that can be tested over a long period and eventually produce profit.

That thought was the starting point of this project.

For now, the principles of stock investing are:

1. Do not lose money.
2. Select stocks that trend upward over the long term by analyzing social trends, company value, news, financial statements, charts, and other signals together.
3. Reduce risk through diversification.

From my perspective, these three principles are the most important, and AI should be designed around them.
